4. Automated Processing in Our Discord Server

Our Discord server uses an AI assistant powered by Anthropic. Messages posted in channels where the assistant is active are sent to Anthropic so it can generate a reply. This means your Discord messages may be processed by a third party located outside the UK.

⚠️ If you would rather your messages were not processed by AI, do not post in channels where the assistant is active. These channels are clearly marked.

The AI assistant does not:

  • Make any decision that produces a legal effect for you
  • Significantly affect you in any meaningful way
  • Decide access, pricing, or anything about your account
  • Store your messages beyond what is necessary for the conversation

The assistant is purely for community engagement and support purposes.

6. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We use a minimal number of cookies to make our site work properly.

Essential Cookies (Required)

These are necessary for the site to function and cannot be switched off:

  • Authentication cookies — remember that you're logged in
  • Session cookies — maintain your session as you navigate the site
  • Security cookies — help protect against cross-site request forgery
  • Preference cookies — remember your theme preference (light/dark mode)

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the user experience. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors by name.

Third-Party Cookies

Some of our providers may set their own cookies when you interact with their services:

  • Stripe — for payment security and fraud prevention
  • Supabase — for authentication tokens

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Allow only first-party cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Note: Blocking essential cookies may affect how our site functions, including your ability to log in or make purchases.

For more information on managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.

8. Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect it.

Technical Measures

  • Encryption in transit — All data transmitted to and from our website is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS)
  • Encryption at rest — Sensitive data stored in our database is encrypted
  • Secure authentication — We use industry-standard authentication protocols and secure password hashing
  • Access controls — Only authorized personnel have access to personal data, on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular updates — We keep our systems and software up to date with security patches

Payment Security

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of certification in the payment card industry. We never see, store, or have access to your full card number.

Third-Party Security

We carefully select providers who maintain high security standards. Our key providers (Stripe, Supabase, Netlify) all maintain SOC 2 compliance and industry-standard security certifications.

Incident Response

In the unlikely event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the ICO within 72 hours where required
  • Notify affected individuals without undue delay if there is a high risk
  • Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach

⚠️ While we implement robust security measures, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and using a strong, unique password.

9. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access — Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification — Have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed
  • Right to Erasure — Ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances (e.g., data no longer needed, consent withdrawn)
  • Right to Restriction — Ask us to limit how we use your data while a complaint is resolved
  • Right to Data Portability — Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — We do not make significant decisions about you using solely automated means

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, email support@quantprime.uk with "Data Rights Request" in the subject line.

We will:

  • Respond within one month (we may extend this by up to two months for complex requests)
  • Verify your identity before processing your request
  • Not charge you a fee (unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive)
  • Not treat you differently for exercising your rights

Right to Complain

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Phone: 0303 123 1113

We would appreciate the chance to address your concerns before you contact the ICO, so please reach out to us first.

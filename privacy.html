4. Automated Processing in Our Discord Server

Our Discord server uses an AI assistant. Messages posted in channels where the assistant is active are sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, so it can generate a reply. This means your Discord messages may be processed by a third party outside the UK.

If you would rather this did not happen, do not post in channels where the assistant is active.

The assistant does not make any decision that produces a legal effect for you or similarly significantly affects you. It does not decide access, pricing, or anything about your account.

Several of our providers are based outside the UK, as shown above. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, to make sure it stays protected to UK standards.

You can ask us for more detail about the safeguards for any particular transfer.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, in certain circumstances
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on it

To exercise any of these, email support@quantprime.uk. We will respond within one month. We will not charge you, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

8. How Long We Keep It

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data: For as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period after closure
  • Email subscriber data: Until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record to prevent re-adding you
  • Payment records: As required by UK tax law (typically 6 years)
  • Server logs: Typically 90 days
  • Support correspondence: 2 years after resolution

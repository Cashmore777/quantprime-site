Meridian Operator Guide

A reversal instrument. It tracks the accumulation, manipulation and distribution rhythm underneath the session, and marks conditions when a level is swept and price rejects it.

Overview

Meridian tracks the AMD rhythm. It does not tell you to buy or sell. It marks conditions where a level has been swept and price rejects it, which historically precedes a reversal.

What It Measures

The market moves through repeating phases:

  • Accumulation — price ranges and positions get built
  • Manipulation — it pushes through an obvious level to take out the orders resting there
  • Distribution — it actually moves

Meridian tracks that rhythm on three cycle lengths: 12 hour, 6 hour and 3 hour. Only the 12 hour cycle is enabled by default.

Each cycle leaves behind A-lines, which mark the highs and lows of its accumulation phase. Those are the levels the instrument watches, because they are where the obvious stops sit.

How Conditions Resolve

A condition resolves when two things happen in order:

  1. An A-line gets swept, which sets the directional bias
  2. Then price taps a vortex level, which marks the entry

The sweep decides the direction. The tap decides the moment.

Why Cycles Are Anchored

The cycles anchor to a timestamp origin at midnight in the chosen timezone, not to a window that rolls with the current bar.

That difference matters more than it sounds. A rolling window puts the phase boundaries wherever you happen to be looking. An anchored one puts them where everybody else's are too—and the entire point is to be positioned where the liquidity actually sits.

Meridian marks conditions. You decide. Nothing here is a signal to follow blindly.

Setup

1

Add to TradingView

Open Indicators, then Invite-only scripts. Find Quant Prime — Meridian and add it to your chart.

2

Understand the Two Timezone Settings

This catches people out. Timezone (cycle anchor) in the AMD section defaults to UTC+1 and decides where the AMD cycles start counting. Timezone in the Time Windows section defaults to UTC and only applies to trading windows. They are deliberately different.

3

Leave the Defaults Alone

These are the configuration actually traded. Change nothing for the first two weeks.

4

Run It Alone First

If you also have Recoil, take Recoil off the chart to begin with. One instrument at a time until you know what each does on its own.

5

Learn to See the Sweep Manually

Before relying on the marks, go back through a day and find a sweep yourself. An A-line that price pushed through and came back from. Once you can spot it by eye, the instrument stops being a black box.

Configuration

These are the settings that matter. Each is shown with the default value actually traded.

AMD Engine On

The whole cycle layer. Off, and there are no A-lines and no conditions.

Timezone (Cycle Anchor) UTC+1

The single most consequential setting in the instrument, and the first thing to check if Meridian appears not to work on your pair. Every phase boundary moves with it, which means every A-line and every condition moves with it.

Cycles 12H on, 6H and 3H off

All three can run at once. Only the 12 hour is enabled by default because it produced the results the defaults are based on. The 6H and 3H cycles produce more conditions and finer structure. Turn them on one at a time, and give each a fortnight before deciding.

Vortex Digit families rotating hourly

Once an A-line has been swept and a bias is set, the vortex layer decides which price levels count as a tap. It works on digits, and it changes every hour.

Active Family Hours (UTC)
3, 6, 9 00, 03, 06, 09, 12, 15, 18, 21
1, 4, 7 01, 04, 07, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22
2, 5, 8 02, 05, 08, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23
Time Windows 00:00–02:00, 04:00–06:00, 19:00–20:00 UTC

Conditions only resolve inside these hours. These are not Recoil's windows and they should not be. The two instruments trigger on different conditions and those conditions cluster at different times.

Exit Mode Opposite Prime Only

Positions close when an opposite condition appears, and the whole position flips. This is a bias decision rather than a stop-loss decision.

Stacking Enabled

Meridian stacks. Same-direction conditions mark additional positions rather than being ignored, and there is no limit on how many. Size each entry so the full stack sits within what you would have risked on one trade, not each position within it.

Usage

Where Meridian Works

Meridian identifies reversals at swept structure. It works best in:

  • Sessions with clean liquidity pools — obvious swing highs/lows that get taken
  • High-liquidity pairs like EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY
  • Lower timeframes where the phase structure has enough bars to form

Where Meridian Fails

Critical Failure Mode

It stops working above the 15 minute chart. Not degrades. Stops. The cycles need a certain number of bars of structure inside each phase before a condition can resolve, and on 15M and above those bars do not exist. If you were planning to take it to 1H because the marks look cleaner up there, do not.

  • Drawdown is roughly seven times Recoil's. Backtested over six months on EURUSD: maximum drawdown 11.59% against Recoil's 1.57%. Profit factor is higher (1.995 vs 1.657), so it earns the risk, but the ride is not comparable.
  • It triggers infrequently. Recoil fires on a candle, so it happens often. Meridian needs a sweep, a tap and the right window, so it happens rarely. Your sample builds slowly.

Test It on Your Own Instrument

  • Sweep Report — how often a swept level produces a rejection rather than a continuation
  • Phase Report — which AMD phase actually produces movement on your instrument
  • Session Report — the three windows against every hour outside them

Give these more history than you would give Recoil's. Meridian reaches statistical significance considerably more slowly.

Troubleshooting

What You See Usually Means
Almost nothing marked Cycle anchor timezone. Check it before anything else.
Phases offset from the session Cycle anchor again.
A-lines but no conditions Prime signals disabled, or outside time windows
Position larger than expected The stack. Several same-direction entries are open.
Everything closed and reversed at once Working as designed. Opposite condition flips the whole position.
Indicator not appearing TradingView username mismatch. Check your dashboard for the linked account.
Conditions resolving on 1H Use 15M or below. The instrument does not work on higher timeframes.

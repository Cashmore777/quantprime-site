[SLIDE: "PART 2: THE MYTHS" - with myth/crossed out visual]

[SLIDE: Myth #1 - "It's just random" with X through it]

**Myth: "There is no algorithm. It's just random."**

I can tell you with certainty that there IS an algorithm. The people who compare trading to gambling simply don't understand how it works. They assume.

[SLIDE: Algorithm visualization - flowing mathematical patterns]

There is a strict algorithmic structure that the entire market runs on. Throughout this education, I'm going to teach you techniques to take profit by following that algorithm as it moves.

This isn't going to be "here's a strategy, this is how it works, good luck." That's not how the founder approaches education. This is a completely unique approach in the forex industry.

[SLIDE: Myth #2 - "It's too hard" with X through it]

**Myth: "Forex is too hard."**

[SLIDE: Maze transforming into clear path]

It's only hard because you don't know where to look or how to start in a structured way. The process itself isn't hard... it's time-consuming and requires patience.

The only thing that's truly hard is overcoming your own mind and your own belief systems.

[SLIDE: Key unlocking door visual]

If I were to introduce you to the true reality of how the algorithm works... it would not be hard.

[SLIDE: Myth #3 - "This strategy is the best" with X through it]

**Myth: "This strategy is the best."**

[SLIDE: Multiple strategies converging into one algorithm]

All strategies work as well as each other. What truly matters is how much attention you give to improving YOUR strategy and sharpening YOUR edge.

All strategies sit inside of the algorithm. They're all part of one overarching algorithmic flow. No one strategy is better than another... the only thing that's better is how refined and upgraded your approach has become.

[SLIDE: Two traders with different charts - same results]

If I tell you I have the algorithm, it's because I've mastered the exact way that I perceive the market. I know when and where things happen. But someone else on my level could also know when and where... from a completely different approach based on their perception.

[SLIDE: Myth #4 - "Make 10k today" with X through it]

**Myth: "Make 10k today."**

[SLIDE: Marketing hype dissolving into reality]

This is pure marketing. There's no way to instantly get rich from trading.

To make significant money in a day requires a fully automated system that you can plug into. That's what the Quant Prime instruments provide... but even then, it takes time to build.

[SLIDE: Risk calculation visual - 1% → returns]

Forex is based on risk. If you risk 1% of a hundred pounds, you're risking one pound. Potentially making two to five pounds depending on the risk-to-reward ratio.

With the right approach, one-to-ten risk-to-reward setups happen regularly. That one pound becomes ten... multiple times per day. As your account grows, so does that percentage.

Do the maths.

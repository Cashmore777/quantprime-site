### Start With A Clean Slate

Approach forex as a blank canvas.

If you've traded before — whether successfully or not — it's important to temporarily set aside your past lessons, wins, and failures.

This isn't about dismissing your experience. It's about creating space for a new perspective.

The Quant Prime approach may challenge some of what you've previously learned. That's intentional. Many common "strategies" taught online are incomplete at best, misleading at worst.

Come into this with a student mindset. Don't talk over your teacher. Leave your ego at the door.

The members who progress fastest are the ones who remain open to seeing things differently.

---

### Forget About The Money — For Now

This might sound counterintuitive, but it's crucial.

Forget about your goals of making huge sums of money. At least for now.

When you start trading with dollar signs in your eyes, you make emotional decisions. You overtrade. You over-leverage. You take setups that don't meet your criteria because you're chasing a number.

That's how accounts get blown.

Instead, focus entirely on learning. Focus on understanding how the market moves. Focus on mastering the concepts being taught.

Balance growth will come later. Profits will come later.

Right now, your only job is to learn.

### The Tools You Need

To follow along with this academy and trade effectively, you'll need a few essential tools:

**TradingView** — This is your charting platform. It's where you'll analyze the markets, mark up levels, and identify setups. The free version works fine to start, but the paid plans offer more features if you want them later.

**MetaTrader 5** — This is your execution platform. It's where you actually place trades, manage positions, and connect to your broker. Most brokers support MT5, and it's the industry standard for a reason.

**A Lot Size Calculator** — This is essential for risk management. Before every trade, you need to know exactly how much you're risking. There are free apps and websites that do this calculation instantly.

These three tools form the foundation. Get comfortable with them. Learn how they work. Practice navigating them until it feels natural.

They will become your second home.

### Building Your Routine

Consistency beats intensity.

You don't need to spend ten hours a day on charts — especially at the beginning. What you need is a routine that gives you dedicated time every single day to develop your knowledge.

Even thirty minutes to an hour of focused learning is enough. Watch these modules. Take notes. Review what you've learned. Apply it on demo.

The key is daily commitment. Small, consistent actions compound into massive results over time.

Think of it like going to the gym. One intense session won't transform your body. But showing up every day, putting in the work, staying disciplined — that's what creates change.

Trading is the same.

### Your First Practical Tasks

Before moving on to the next level, there are a few things to complete:

**Task 1: Download the tools.**
Get TradingView on your phone and computer. Download MetaTrader 5. Find a lot size calculator that works for you.

**Task 2: Create your accounts.**
Set up a demo account with your chosen broker. This is where you'll practice without risking real money.

**Task 3: Explore the platforms.**
Spend time clicking around. Open charts. Place demo trades. Get familiar with where everything is.

**Task 4: Practice measuring pips.**
On TradingView, use the long or short position tool to measure distances on the chart. Understanding pips is fundamental — it's how risk and reward are calculated.

Don't rush through these. The members who take time to properly set up their foundation are the ones who succeed long-term.

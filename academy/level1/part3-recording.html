How To Get Started

Setting up your foundation properly

🧠
Start With A Clean Slate
Approach forex as a blank canvas.

If you've traded before, temporarily set aside your past lessons, wins, and failures. This creates space for a new perspective.

Leave your ego at the door. The members who progress fastest are the ones who remain open.
💭
Forget About The Money — For Now
When you start trading with dollar signs in your eyes, you make emotional decisions. You overtrade. You over-leverage.

That's how accounts get blown.

Right now, your only job is to learn. Profits will come later.

Choosing A Broker

Quant Prime does not operate as a broker affiliate. No hidden commission. No kickbacks.

Focus on: best spreads, highest quality execution, reliable withdrawals.

Do your own research. Find a broker that's regulated and reputable.

The Tools You Need

📊
TradingView
Your charting platform. Analyze markets, mark levels, identify setups.
⚡
MetaTrader 5
Your execution platform. Place trades, manage positions.
🧮
Lot Size Calculator

Building Your Routine
Consistency beats intensity.

Even 30 minutes to an hour of focused learning every day is enough. Watch modules. Take notes. Apply on demo.

Think of it like the gym. One intense session won't transform you. Showing up every day does.

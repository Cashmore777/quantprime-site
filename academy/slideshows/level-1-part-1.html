Understanding Risk

Risk management is the foundation of longevity.

0.25 - 0.5% Conservative Building consistency
0.5 - 1.5% Moderate Most developing traders
1.5 - 3% Elevated Experienced only
3%+ Aggressive Accelerates gains and losses

Choose a risk level and commit to it. Consistency allows edge to compound.

Fundamentals

Measuring the Market

A pip is the standard unit of measurement in forex trading. It tells you how far price has moved.

Stop Loss + Risk % = Position Size

TradingView has built-in tools that display pip distance automatically. Use them.

Most Pairs 4th Decimal 1.2345
JPY Pairs 2nd Decimal 152.45

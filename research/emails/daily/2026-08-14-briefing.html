UNDERSTANDING REGIME SCORES
Regime scores measure trading conditions based on two key factors:
Volatility (ATR Ratio) — Is price movement within normal range? Scores highest when ATR is 85-120% of its 50-period average.
Trend Strength (ADX) — How strong is the directional movement? Higher ADX = stronger trend.
Prime (70+) — Optimal. Trade confidently.
Favourable (55-70) — Good. Proceed normally.
Marginal (40-55) — Caution. Reduce size.
Degraded (<40) — Poor. Consider avoiding.
Daily Bias is determined by EMA alignment on the 4H timeframe. This is directional context, not a trade signal.

Questions

How does the free trial work?

Start Research free for 30 days. Card is required upfront to prevent abuse. If it's not for you, cancel before day 30 and pay nothing. After the trial, you're charged £8/month. Cancel anytime with one click.

What are build combinations?

Each system has 12 modular pieces you can combine in 255 different ways. 4 build menus × 255 combinations = 1020+ ways to build exactly what suits your trading style.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes, anytime. Upgrades are prorated — you only pay the difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Your access adjusts immediately.

How do the TradingView indicators work?

After purchase, share your TradingView username. We grant access within 24 hours (usually faster). They appear in your "Invite-only scripts" section. Works on any TradingView plan.

What's the refund policy?

All paid tiers have a 14-day money-back guarantee. If the indicators aren't for you, email us within 14 days for a full refund. No questions asked.

Do I keep the research papers if I cancel?

Papers are part of your active subscription. If you cancel, you lose access at the end of your billing period. But you can always re-subscribe to regain access.

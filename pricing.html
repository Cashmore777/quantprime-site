Common questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the next billing cycle.

How do I access the indicators?

After purchase, you'll provide your TradingView username. We grant access within 24 hours. The indicators appear in your TradingView Invite-only scripts.

Is there a refund policy?

Annual plans have a 14-day refund window if you haven't accessed premium content. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime but aren't refunded mid-cycle.

What's the Completion Guarantee?

Ascension members who complete the full curriculum and don't see improvement after 90 days of documented trading can request a full refund.
